# Road Guess — Android Game

Road Guess is an Android game that challenges players to test their knowledge of their own city. Players are presented with an overhead map of the city, where a particular road is highlighted, and must guess its name before the timer runs out. The game turns everyday geography into a fast-paced challenge, rewarding familiarity with local streets, spatial awareness, and quick thinking. Its simple premise makes it easy to pick up while leaving room for competition and replayability as players try to improve their scores.

<br>

The app uses Mapbox for map integration, providing the geographic visualization that forms the foundation of the gameplay. The main challenge is building an engaging experience around the map, combining road highlighting, player input, and a countdown timer into a smooth and intuitive game loop. The Android interface is designed to keep the focus on the map and the guessing challenge, making each round a race to recognize the highlighted road before time runs out.

<br>

Road Guess is still under active development and is currently in the playtesting phase. This stage focuses on evaluating the core gameplay, refining the user experience, and gathering feedback on the difficulty, pacing, and overall feel of the game. The project is an ongoing exploration of mobile game development and map-based interaction, with the goal of turning knowledge of familiar streets into an accessible, competitive, and enjoyable game.
